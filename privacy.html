Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026  ·  tack  ·  tack.design

Overview

tack ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website and Chrome extension at tack.design. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you use tack and how it is used.

By using tack, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Process

When you use tack, we process the information needed to run the product, authenticate your account, and save your usage history when that feature is enabled.

  • Account information — if you create an account, we process your email address, authentication details, and plan status.
  • Reference image data — when you open the extension on a page, tack reads image URLs and basic image metadata in your browser so you can choose references. tack currently includes direct support for tack.design, Pinterest, Instagram, Behance, and Dribbble, and may also read image data from the current tab when you explicitly open the extension on another regular image-based page. The image URLs and related image content you select are processed so tack can analyze the shared visual style.
  • Your prompt — the subject or request you type is processed to generate new images in the selected style.
  • Local page context — the current page URL and workspace state may be stored locally in your browser so tack can restore your last scan or save generation history on your device.
  • Generated images and history — if you are signed in, generated images and related metadata may be stored so they can appear in your history across devices.
  • Billing information — if you upgrade, billing is processed by Stripe. We do not store full payment card details on our own servers.

We keep only the information needed to operate the service, support your account, enforce plan limits, and provide features like saved history and billing management. tack does not use advertising trackers to profile your browsing.

Third-Party Services

tack is built on third-party AI and hosting services. Your inputs are sent to these providers to generate results:

  • Anthropic — used for style analysis and prompt construction. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Replicate / Black Forest Labs — used to generate images from the prepared prompt and style direction. Replicate Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — used for authentication, account data, and saved history. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — used for checkout, subscriptions, and billing portal access. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Vercel — hosts this website and its serverless functions. Vercel may log standard request metadata such as IP address and timestamp. Vercel Privacy Policy

Cookies & Tracking

tack does not use advertising trackers on the marketing site. We may use essential browser storage or authentication state to keep you signed in and provide account features. Third-party services we use, such as Stripe or Supabase, may also use technically necessary cookies or storage in the course of providing their services.

Children's Privacy

tack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy
