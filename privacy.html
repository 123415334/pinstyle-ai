Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2025  ·  PinStyle AI  ·  pinstyle-ai.vercel.app

Overview

PinStyle AI ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at pinstyle-ai.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you use the service and how it is used.

By using PinStyle AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Process

We do not create user accounts or store personal data on our servers. When you use PinStyle AI, the following information is temporarily processed to deliver the service:

  • Pinterest board URL — the URL you paste into the tool, used to analyze the board's visual style.
  • Your prompt — the description of what you want to create, passed to our AI providers.
  • Generated images — AI-generated images are hosted by OpenAI and served directly to your browser. We do not store copies of generated images.

No input or output data is stored in a database or retained after your session ends.

Third-Party Services

PinStyle AI is built on third-party AI and hosting services. Your inputs are sent to these providers to generate results:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — processes your board URL and prompt to extract visual style and generate image prompts. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI (DALL-E 3) — generates images from the style prompt. OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Pinterest — board URLs you provide may be fetched to extract visual style information. Pinterest's own privacy policy governs their platform.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and its serverless functions. Vercel may log standard request metadata such as IP address and timestamp. Vercel Privacy Policy

Cookies & Tracking

PinStyle AI does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any form of user tracking. We do not use Google Analytics or similar tools.

Children's Privacy

PinStyle AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
