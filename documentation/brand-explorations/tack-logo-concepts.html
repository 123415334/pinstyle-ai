Notes for development: Concepts A, B, and F share the same grid DNA as the original and would feel like an evolution rather than a pivot. Concept C (wordmark) and G (ultra-light) are the highest-risk, highest-reward — they rely entirely on type but scale to any size. The T monogram (E) is worth developing as a secondary mark / favicon regardless of which direction wins.
