How it works

01

Browse the web.
Select what fits.

Open tack on the visual inspiration sites you already use, from moodboards and portfolios to product pages and image searches. On supported pages, select the images you want to use as references right where you found them.

02

Describe what
to create.

Describe what you want to create: an illustration, a photograph, an object, a campaign visual. Tack keeps the style from your references and applies it to something new.

03

Generate your
images.

Generate new images that carry the visual language from your references into the subject you described. Tack keeps the style analysis and prompt details close to the results.

04

Generations saved
to your tack account.

Tack automatically saves every generation to your account page, where you can revisit results, download favorites, and organize the keepers into boards you can reuse as future style references.
