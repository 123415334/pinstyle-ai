We made a few big updates to Tack, and the main one is simple: it now works across much more of the web.

Any compatible site, fewer restrictions. You can collect references wherever you find them, then use those images to generate new visuals in the same style.

Here's what changed:

  • Works across more of the web, so you can use Tack on more reference-rich pages.
  • Upgraded boards, with a tighter flow between saved references and new generations.
  • Dimension controls, so you can generate square, vertical, or horizontal images.
  • Bug fixes, including improved page scans, fewer generation failures, and cleaner behavior throughout.

If you want to try it, open Tack on a page with images you like, save a few to a board, choose your dimensions, and generate something new from that visual direction.
